01 / THE ROAD

The road to enlightenment

The web has travelled a long way from rigid tables and browser-specific tricks. Today, thoughtful standards let structure and presentation work together: clear words underneath, an open field for design above.

02 / THE IDEA

So, what is this about?

This page is a small tribute to the CSS Zen Garden challenge. Its central idea is simple and generous: keep the HTML, change the CSS, and discover how differently the same content can feel. Choose a style above to see this page shift without losing a word.

03 / TAKE PART

Participation

Start with the structure, then make the visual decisions your own. In the garden above, rake the gravel or place a stone or moss. Here, the four design studies offer different starting points for the same content.

04 / WHY DESIGN

Benefits

Design is a way of seeing what a page can become. These studies celebrate the craft of CSS, make room for experimentation, and invite people to learn from one another's work.

05 / GOOD PRACTICE

Requirements

A beautiful page should still be readable, responsive, and easy to navigate. This garden keeps its tools labelled, works with a keyboard, and respects reduced-motion preferences along the way.
